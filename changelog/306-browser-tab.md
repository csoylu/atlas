## Added · #306 · The chat's browser is a tab in the pane: an address bar, who is driving with Take control and Hand back, the agent's clicks shown on the page, and every state of the mockup

- **Bar:** back, forward, reload (Stop while loading), the address (typed addresses get http:// for local hosts), and the driver: « atlas is driving » + Take control, or « You're driving » + Hand back to atlas.
- **What the agent does shows:** a pulse on the spot and a caption (« paris clicked « Search » »; a typed value is never shown), an accent edge around the page, a live dot on the tab, and a dot on the Documents button while the pane is hidden.
- **Control:** a click on the page while the agent is idle takes control; while it is acting, the click is refused and the bar says so. While you drive, the agent's calls fail at once with « Master is using the browser »; after Hand back its next call starts with the page as you left it.
- **States:** empty (address focused, recent pages), loading, opened from a link, login wall (« atlas needs you »), blocked by the workspace's network setting, offline, crashed, and the web UI (the browser runs in the desktop app).
- **Links** clicked in a chat or the task beside it open in that chat's browser; ⌘-click, downloads and sign-in pages still go to the system browser.
- **Comments** on a page (comment mode): the page is frozen on a picture; a click names the element, an area is cropped from that picture. « What the agent sees » names the page's title and address.
