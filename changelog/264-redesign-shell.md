## Changed · #264 · The app shell follows the redesign: a ⌘K button in the sidebar, folding project groups, and a bottom tab bar instead of the menu drawer on narrow windows.

- Sidebar: the ⌘K button next to the Atlas name opens search and commands; each project's group of agents folds with
  its chevron (kept per project); History stays under Running.
- 900 px and narrower: no sidebar and no hamburger drawer; a tab bar at the bottom (Inbox, Tasks, Chats, Library,
  More). Chats lists the agents by project and the chat history; More holds Workflows, the running tasks, the
  update and secrets badges, you and Settings. A chat, a workflow and every settings page get a back arrow.
- ⌘K palette: results carry their project tag, and « Switch to project … » sets the project filter (with two projects
  or more); no key hints on a phone.
