## Changed · #371 · The Tasks board takes the obsidian look: columns cut by thin lines, a glowing edge on tasks at work, blocked tasks framed in red with their question underneath.

Second of the four #362 redesign tasks.
- Columns run edge to edge, separated by 1 px lines, under a 40 px header line; empty or folded columns stay 40 px rails with their count.
- Cards: number and time in the column on top, the title, then avatar, project and priority bars.
- Running: an accent edge with a glint running down it and the live time in the accent (on an accent fill in light).
- Blocked: a red border and a red footer with what it needs and the question. A waiting Todo shows « waits for #N » as its footer.
- Done and Closed recede: no surface, grey titles.
- Header tabs show their counts; « Needs you » turns red when something waits on you. Phone: the status strip is a row of cells underlined in the accent.
